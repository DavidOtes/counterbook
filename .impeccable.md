# Impeccable — project design file

## Design Context

### Users
Small-business owners — shopkeepers, phone/laptop repairers, tailors, spare-
parts sellers — mostly on budget Android phones, often in bright daylight
shops or market stalls, frequently on unreliable connections. Many currently
keep records in a paper bookkeeping book. Low patience for setup; records
happen mid-transaction with a customer standing there.

### Job to be done
Record a sale and hand over a receipt in under ten seconds; know "how much
did I make today" and "who owes me" without computing anything; keep stock
counts honest without stocktaking discipline.

### Brand Personality
**Practical, warm, trustworthy** — like a well-kept ledger book. Not corporate
SaaS, not fintech-neon. The interface should feel like stationery you respect:
ruled lines, stamped totals, carbon-copy receipts.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light theme only (daylight use is the context; dark mode is not a goal).
- Warm paper surfaces (`--color-paper`), deep market-green brand
  (`--color-brand`), amber for money owed, clay for danger. All OKLCH,
  neutrals tinted toward the brand hue.
- Type: **Bricolage Grotesque** (display, big totals), **Hanken Grotesk**
  (UI text), **Spline Sans Mono** (money and receipts — always; tabular
  figures are non-negotiable in a ledger). All bundled via @fontsource for
  offline.
- Signature elements: hairline-ruled list rows (`.ledger-row`), the thermal-
  receipt detail view with dashed rules and a rotated PAID stamp, money in
  the receipt face everywhere.
- Anti-references: generic admin dashboards, card grids with icon+heading,
  cyan-on-dark AI slop, gradient text, side-stripe alert borders.

### Design Principles
1. **Ten seconds to a receipt.** Every added tap in the sale flow must justify
   itself; defaults (walk-in customer, paid-now cash) carry the common case.
2. **Structure is opt-in.** Free-text lines and nameless customers are valid;
   the app offers to save things after use, never demands setup before it.
3. **Never block the sale.** Warnings over walls: negative stock warns, syncing
   never spins a blocker, receipts render offline.
4. **The number the owner needs is already computed.** Today's total, who owes
   what — headline answers, not charts. (Stat tiles over sparkline decoration.)
5. **Money is sacred text.** Receipt mono face, tabular numerals, minor-unit
   integers underneath — amounts never wrap, jitter, or round oddly.
